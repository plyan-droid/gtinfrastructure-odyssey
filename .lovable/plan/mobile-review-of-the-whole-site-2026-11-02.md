# Mobile review of the whole site

## What I will check
Open the site on phone sizes (small phone 360x740, iPhone 390x844, large phone 430x932) and step through every story section from the opening screen to the closing screen, plus every Read More popup.

For each screen I will look for:
- Words overlapping the map, other words, or the buttons
- Text that is too small to read or runs off the edge
- Map boxes (including the wider AI / Agent / LLM Service box) cut off or crowded
- Info boxes (like DETECT) covering map boxes
- Popups: charts and tables fit or scroll sideways cleanly, video player controls fit, close button reachable
- Closing screen: the three steps, the 3.9 man-days row and its info tap, and both buttons
- Site Map menu opens and jumps to each section

## What I will fix
Any problem found, using phone-only adjustments so the desktop and TV look stays the same. Typical fixes: spacing, font size, map framing on phone, chart layout stacking, table scrolling.

## How I will confirm
Screenshot every section and popup before and after fixes, re-run the site's checks, and report back what was fixed and anything still worth your decision.
